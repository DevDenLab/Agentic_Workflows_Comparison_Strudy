v2's confidence gate is stricter than it needs to be. Of the in-distribution tickets it sent to a human instead of auto-resolving, 20 of 23 already had the correct category, priority and team in the draft.

The pattern: the agent cites rule:R021, then writes a rationale like “blocked imaging access delaying patient care” — true, but not literally stated in the rule match — and the grounding critic rejects it as unsupported. A real, reported limitation, not tuned away.
