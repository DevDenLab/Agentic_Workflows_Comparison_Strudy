# Runbook: clinical fleet devices

## Barcode scanners not reading wristbands
1. Confirm the wristband label itself is legible and not printed faint (see the wristband printer
   section below); a scanner fault and a bad label look identical to the end user.
2. Re-pair the scanner to its base station: hold the trigger for 5 seconds until it beeps twice.
3. If several scanners on the same unit fail at once, the base station or its network drop is the
   more likely cause than the individual scanners; escalate as a unit-wide issue.

## Workstation on wheels (WOW) / medication cart problems
- Won't power on: check the cart is docked and the dock's indicator light is green, not amber.
- Battery dies within an hour: batteries are rated for an 8-hour shift; anything under 3 hours is a
  battery replacement, not a software issue.
- Shuts off mid-use: this is almost always thermal shutdown from a blocked vent on the cart, not the
  computer itself. Confirm nothing is stacked on top of the cart.

## Wristband / label printers
Blank or faded output on an otherwise-working printer is a ribbon or label-stock issue, not a
software fault. Confirm ribbon orientation before escalating a repair ticket.
